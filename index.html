SHEET 1 — ABOUT

About

Hands-on fabrication, self-taught electronics.

I'm Adedoyin — a fabrication and electronics technician based in Lagos. Day to day, I run CO₂ laser cutting and 3D printing production, and I take on freelance work preparing fabrication-ready files and repairing electronics on the side.

Driven by a deep fascination with how physical hardware comes together, I transitioned from tinkering with circuits to a formal grounding in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at Yaba College of Technology. Practical industrial placements and hands-on fabrication floors turned that theoretical foundation into real-world competency with automated machinery and rapid prototyping. Today, that background fuels a hands-on approach to building both functional engineering systems and clean digital products.

  • Laser cutting 150W CO₂, LightBurn & RDWorks, kerf compensation, nesting
  • 3D printing Bambu Lab A1 (FDM), Elegoo Saturn 4 (SLA)
  • Electronics Component-level repair, KiCad PCB design, Arduino / STM32 / ESP32
  • File prep DXF / SVG cleanup, layer color-coding, material optimization
